ACME-HOME is a private home-server system for one owner.
- A small server runs Docker services (files, photos, passwords, a local LLM) behind a private VPN mesh.
- A team of AI agents plans, builds and reviews changes. Third-party code is cloned, scanned and reviewed before any install.
- Interface: one Telegram bot. Inbox notes, daily nudges, video notes from shared links, a library of books.
- Rules: self-hosted and free first; no secrets in prompts; the owner approves every install.
